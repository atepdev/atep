Why ATEP

A spoofed or altered command to a robot can cause physical harm, and fleets are now mixed: machines from several vendors have to coordinate while each vendor has its own closed security. ATEP gives every message a verifiable origin, integrity and certification record that works across vendors. The protocol itself lives on atep.dev.

Why not just TLS?

TLS secures a link. It does not say whether the sender may issue a motion command, whether it is certified, or whether it has been revoked, and it does not survive relays, brokers or store-and-forward links. ATEP protects the message itself and can be used on top of TLS.

Why open?

A trust layer that every vendor must adopt cannot belong to one vendor. The specification is CC BY 4.0 and the code is Apache-2.0, anyone may implement it, and verifiers choose their own roots. Our commercial plans sit around the protocol, not inside it.

Why quantum-safe now?

Signed safety records and encrypted traffic recorded today must hold for a decade or more. Hybrid signatures and key exchange cost little now and avoid a forced migration later.

What it will not become

ATEP is not a reputation or scoring system for people. There is no global score, no mandatory root, and no surveillance feed. These commitments are written into the specification.

AIRAD LABS creates and stewards the open protocol ATEP (atep.dev). No customer, certification or hosted service is claimed on this site; everything under Products is planned unless it says otherwise.
