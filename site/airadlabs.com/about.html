About AIRAD LABS

AIRAD LABS is the company that created ATEP, the Autonomy Trust Envelope Protocol, and stewards its specification and reference implementations. It intends to operate the first public transparency log and root issuer, run a certification program, and sell tooling to fleet operators.

The protocol is deliberately kept on a neutral domain, atep.dev, so that competing vendors can adopt it without pointing at us. The specification is CC BY 4.0, the code Apache-2.0, and a registry that breaks the published design commitments loses the right to use the ATEP name.

This is an early-stage project. The reference implementations and the demo are built; commercial services are not yet offered.

AIRAD LABS creates and stewards the open protocol ATEP (atep.dev). No customer, certification or hosted service is claimed on this site; everything under Products is planned unless it says otherwise.
