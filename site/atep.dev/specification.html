Specification, Draft 07

Draft 07 of the Autonomy Trust Envelope Protocol specification, a trust layer built on hybrid classical and NIST post-quantum cryptography, is dated 2 October 2026 and is the first public draft (Drafts 00 to 06 were internal working drafts). The complete text is available as plain Markdown in one file, llms-full.txt, which is generated from spec/ATEP-Specification-Draft-07.md in the repository by a script and prefixed with a short note describing it.

Published drafts are never edited: a change is a new draft. A rendered, browsable version of the specification is not part of this site yet. Behavior that the text leaves ambiguous is decided by the test vectors, and the text then follows the vectors.

Claim types

The claim types that attestations name under https://atep.dev/claims/ are defined in section 7 and section 17. Each of the 14 has a page with its definition, who issues it, the CDDL schema of data and its lifetime: see the claim types directory.
