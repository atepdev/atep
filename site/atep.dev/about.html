About

ATEP was created, and is stewarded, by AIRAD LABS. The company maintains the specification and the reference implementations and intends to operate the first public transparency log and root issuer. The protocol, the code and the test vectors are open and anyone may implement them.

The claim namespace lives on this neutral domain rather than under the company, so that vendors can adopt ATEP without pointing at AIRAD LABS. Any commercial services AIRAD LABS builds on ATEP are separate from the protocol and are described, as planned and not available, on the company's own site, airadlabs.com.

ATEP is an open protocol. The specification is licensed CC BY 4.0 and the reference code Apache-2.0. Stewarded by AIRAD LABS (about). Source: github.com/atepdev/atep.
